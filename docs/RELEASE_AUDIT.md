## Material changes

### BaZi

- Month-command adjudication retains every hidden-stem candidate; main qi is a framework, not a fabricated exact human commander.
- Strength exposes season, root, and visible-stem axes while preserving the strict establishment gate. Presence-only damage/rescue cannot become breakage or effective rescue.
- Career/study, wealth/resources, and long-term relationships have result-first topic routes. Axis co-presence is only a clue, never a causal or closed chain.
- Period context can emphasize only a matching natal axis. Mixed layers separate natal-axis emphasis from phase-only Ten Gods. Relationship phases prioritize replayed relations to the day branch; spouse-star convention is optional, disclosed, and secondary.
- Fifteen UTC+08 differential fixtures cover ordinary charts, hour sweep, LiChun/Jingzhe, late Zi, midnight, and Zi-start conventions. Four differences from pinned `chinese-fortune` are explicit rather than silently normalized.

### Zi Wei

- A fixed MIT-licensed Mingyu core commit supplies 55 reproducible predicates and 32 refusal boundaries. Outcome prose and source quotations are removed; fixed locators and attribution remain.
- Pattern evidence is supplemental only. Default output carries neutral matches and coverage; full unmet/refusal/traditional-name detail requires explicit `pattern_detail: "audit"`, while `none` omits it.
- An empty focus palace may use exact opposite-palace major-star names as `context_only`; it cannot import brightness, transformations, auxiliary/pressure stars, or sitting-star language.
- Unknown-time scans do not repeatedly evaluate or expose the pattern catalog. Exported rule and boundary tables are deeply frozen.

## Bounded comparison

Within its installed routes, 0.6.0 is stronger than prompt-only workflows on replay, explicit school/profile scope, refusal boundaries, ordinary-language ordering, and protection against unsupported promotion. Directly adapting Mingyu's permitted core conditions also narrows the prior Zi Wei knowledge gap. Mingyu and other large projects still have greater overall method breadth, BaZi knowledge volume, product surface, and maintenance history.

No inspected repository supplied sufficient public preregistered, blinded, out-of-sample evidence to establish real-world predictive validity. The defensible claim is fewer correctable errors and deeper reproducible traditional-rule handling inside scope—not “proved more accurate” or “best.”

## Known limits

- BaZi still lacks exact human-command day weights, a complete force/position/control/transformation state machine, the full climate exception corpus, all special patterns, and a sourced event map.
- The 55 Zi Wei rules are not a complete all-school corpus; broad Flying/钦天/河洛 techniques, rectification, and flow-month/day/hour timing remain unavailable.
- Western remains tropical whole-sign natal; Tarot, I Ching, and Meihua retain their documented bounded profiles; Liu Yao, Qi Men, and Vedic remain planned.
- There is no independent practitioner sign-off, external-user validity study, or established predictive accuracy.

Any later change to code, lockfiles, schemas, Skill routing, rule packs, release documentation, or packaged resources invalidates this sign-off and requires a rebuilt archive plus clean-extraction verification.
